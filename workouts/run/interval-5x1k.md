# 5×1 km intervaller

- 15 min warm-up

- 10 min cool-down

Pacing: jevne reps — dropp rep hvis form bryter.

Plan B: 4×1 km eller 5×800 m.

TP: `WU 15' / 5x1km @ 10k 90s jog / CD 10'`
